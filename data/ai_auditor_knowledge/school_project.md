# School Project

The **School Project** section provides hands‑on learning resources for users who want to explore AI auditing concepts through guided examples.

## What It Contains
- **Tutorials** – Step‑by‑step walkthroughs that demonstrate how to set up a sample audit, interpret results, and apply remediation.
- **Example Audits** – Pre‑configured audit scenarios (e.g., bias detection on a sentiment model) that users can run with a single click.
- **Assignments** – Small exercises encouraging users to modify prompts or models and observe the impact on audit outcomes.
- **Resources** – Links to external reading material, datasets, and research papers.

## How to Use
1. Navigate to **School Project** from the main dashboard.
2. Choose a tutorial or example audit that matches your interest.
3. Follow the numbered instructions displayed on the page. The assistant will be available via the **Help** button if you need clarification.
4. After completing an assignment, you can view your results in **Audit History**.

The goal of this section is to build intuition around audit categories and best practices before applying them to real projects.
