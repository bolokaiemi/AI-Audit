AI Auditor – School Project

This page presents the school-project component of the AI Auditor V0.1 application. The project demonstrates how artificial intelligence systems, models, prompts, and outputs can be evaluated using a structured AI auditing approach.

Project Overview

AI Auditor V0.1 is an educational AI evaluation and auditing platform designed to help users examine AI systems and identify potential problems related to reliability, safety, transparency, fairness, privacy, security, and responsible AI usage.

The application provides an interface where users can enter information about an AI system, model, prompt, test case, or AI-generated response and perform a structured assessment.

Project Objectives

  • Evaluate AI systems and AI-generated outputs.
  • Identify potential risks and weaknesses in AI applications.
  • Assess prompts and responses for problematic behaviour.
  • Improve transparency and accountability in AI systems.
  • Support responsible and trustworthy AI development.
  • Provide students with practical experience in AI auditing.
  • Demonstrate how AI engineering and software engineering can work together.

What Can Be Audited?

AI Systems

Evaluate the behaviour, purpose, functionality, and potential risks of an AI-based application.

AI Models

Examine the expected behaviour, limitations, reliability, and responsible-use considerations of an AI model.

Prompts

Analyse prompts for clarity, safety, potential bias, ambiguity, security risks, and unintended instructions.

AI Responses

Review AI-generated responses for accuracy, relevance, harmful content, bias, hallucinations, and other quality concerns.

Test Cases

Use structured test cases to examine how an AI system behaves under different inputs and scenarios.

AI Applications

Assess AI-powered applications from a broader software engineering and responsible-AI perspective.

AI Audit Areas

Reliability

Determine whether the AI system produces consistent and dependable results.

Safety

Identify potentially harmful, unsafe, or inappropriate system behaviour.

Fairness & Bias

Examine whether the system may produce unfair, discriminatory, or biased outcomes.

Privacy

Consider how personal, confidential, or sensitive information may be handled by the AI system.

Security

Identify possible prompt-injection, data-leakage, misuse, and other AI-related security risks.

Transparency

Evaluate whether users can understand the purpose, limitations, and expected behaviour of the AI system.

Accountability

Consider who is responsible for the AI system, its decisions, and its outcomes.

Responsible AI

Assess whether the system follows responsible and ethical AI development principles.

How to Use AI Auditor V0.1

  1. Enter the AI information. Provide the AI system, model, prompt, response, or test information that you want to evaluate.
  2. Select an audit area. Choose the appropriate evaluation category, such as safety, reliability, fairness, privacy, security, or transparency.
  3. Start the assessment. Submit the information to the AI Auditor for evaluation.
  4. Review the analysis. Examine the identified risks, observations, weaknesses, and recommendations.
  5. Improve the AI system. Use the audit results to improve prompts, models, application logic, security, and responsible-AI practices.

Students can use the project to understand that developing an AI application does not end when the model produces an answer. AI systems must also be tested, evaluated, monitored, and improved.

Project Conclusion

AI Auditor V0.1 demonstrates how AI systems can be evaluated systematically rather than being treated as black-box technologies. The project combines AI engineering with software development, testing, security, and responsible AI practices.

The ultimate goal is to help developers, students, organisations, and AI users understand the strengths and limitations of AI systems and make better decisions about their development and deployment.
